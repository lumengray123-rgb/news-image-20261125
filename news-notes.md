## 1. LEAD — US proposes new tariffs on 14 economies
- Reuters (via Kitco), FXStreet, AP: USTR proposed new tariff rates on 14 trading partners incl. China, EU, Japan, India. Headline rates 20–40%; capped at 20% for China (40% if the trade truce unravels), 15% for EU/Japan/South Korea/Taiwan/Switzerland, 18% for Cambodia/Indonesia/Malaysia/Thailand/Bangladesh, 20% for Vietnam if a deal is reached (35% if not). Follows an 8-month Section 301 probe into excess industrial capacity; replaces broad duties struck down by the Supreme Court in February. Hearings from 25 January; earliest effect January.

## 3. Pakistan's first astronaut docks at Tiangong
- Centreline, SpacePolicyOnline, Reuters, china-in-space: Shenzhou-24 docked with China's Tiangong station at 11:56 Beijing time, ~3.5h after launching from Jiuquan on a Long March-2F. Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali is the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong; crew also Tang Hongbo and Wu Yi. He stays about a week doing science experiments.
- Sources: https://centreline.com.pk/2026/11/25/pakistan-enters-space-as-shenzhou-24-successfully-docks-with-chinas-space-station/ ; https://spacepolicyonline.com/news/first-pakistani-astronaut-enroute-to-tiangong/
